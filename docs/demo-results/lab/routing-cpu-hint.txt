Routing measurement for the lab-02 fix, 19 Nov 2026.

The lab-02 incident (cpu-throttling, "a busy server starved of CPU") was answered by the Spider
because the classifier saw one event: Readiness probe failed ... connection refused. The watcher
now reads the failing container's CPU limit and request from the pod, and its CPU usage from
metrics-server, and puts them on the incident as `resources`. The classifier prompt says what
to do with them. The 14 saved lab incidents were replayed through the real classifier (Claude
Haiku 4.5 on Amazon Bedrock) by tools/lab/measure_routing.py.

WHAT WAS NOT MEASURED: CPU usage. Nobody has read metrics-server for a lab-02 pod. In
configurations C, D and E the usage is ASSUMED: cpu-throttling at its 20m limit (a busy loop held
to its quota), every other probe container idle at 1m. The limits and requests are real: they
come from the scenarios' own pod specs through the watcher's own function. So these tables show
what the classifier does IF the usage is what is assumed here. They do not show that lab-02 on
the cluster reports that usage. That needs one live run (see the README).

Configurations (3 runs per scenario unless stated; Unhealthy incidents are the only ones with a hint):
  A  new prompt, no hint        (does the new prompt text change anything by itself?)
  B  new prompt, limit and request only, usage unknown
  C  new prompt, limit, request and assumed usage
  D  OLD prompt (the one on the cluster before), same hints as C   (is the data alone enough?)
  E  as C, 10 runs, the six probe scenarios only

Reading it:
  - The CPU limit alone does not fix lab-02 (B: 0/3).
  - The data alone does not fix it either (D: 0/3). The prompt rule is needed.
  - With both, and usage at the limit, lab-02 goes to cpu_memory (C: 3/3, E: 10/10).
  - The network scenarios stay network, including the NetworkPolicy one (C: 3/3, E: 10/10).
  - rollout-stuck-readiness (lab-11) is still not routed to deploy_rollout (E: 1/10). This change
    does not fix it, and in 1 of 10 runs the hint sent it to cpu_memory, which is also wrong.
  - Across the 14 scenarios the totals are 36, 35, 39, 36 of 42. The 1-run differences in A and B
    on scenarios that have no hint (missing-configmap-mount 2/3 in B) are sampling noise.
