How it works

  1. Pick a scenario and press Run. It breaks something small in its own namespace on the live cluster.
  2. The watcher notices, and the crew investigates. Follow the link to the incident to read the evidence.
  3. If you press Approve on the incident, it opens a pull request on a sandbox branch. Nothing is applied to the cluster, and nothing is saved as memory.

One scenario at a time. It is cleaned up by itself after 15 minutes, or when you press Reset.
