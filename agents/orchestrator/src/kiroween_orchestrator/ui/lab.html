How it works

  1. Pick a scenario and press Run. It breaks something small in its own namespace on the live cluster.
  2. The watcher notices, and the crew investigates. Follow the link to the incident to read the evidence.
  3. If the crew proposes a fix, you can press Approve on the incident. It opens a pull request on a sandbox branch. Nothing is applied to the cluster, and nothing is saved as memory. Some failures the crew can explain but not fix by itself, such as a Secret or ConfigMap that does not exist: those end as a diagnosis, with nothing to approve.

Each card names the crew member who should answer. A failed health check looks much the same whatever caused it, so the incident shows who actually did.

One scenario at a time. It is cleaned up by itself after 15 minutes, or when you press Reset.
